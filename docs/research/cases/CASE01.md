Sources: RX16. Read scope: Carlson 2007 chronology and competing SEC/CME/CFTC assessments

Competing explanations and limits: Do not attribute the entire event to portfolio insurance; contemporary inquiries differed and event data do not isolate one cause.

Assessment: Reconstruct a hedge selling into falling prices with finite liquidity; compare a precommitted static protection alternative and its cost.
