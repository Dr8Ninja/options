# Source analysis and item dispositions

The exhaustive [coverage matrix](../../research/registers/coverage-matrix.json) is keyed to every original module, topic, scope, resource, prerequisite, inherited association, stage, assessment, domain and supplemental item, with item-level capstone/branch/decision-card records. Its ratings describe what the source supplies; they do not award lesson quality for a topic name. Each row separates scope, accuracy, teaching, practice, verification and maintenance, and includes provenance and canonical disposition.

Excellent requires reviewed instruction, worked and transfer practice, bounded evidence and current maintenance. Good means coherent scope/design with stated remaining work. Partial means a concept exists but teaching/practice is incomplete. Weak means a mention, inherited citation or unverified annotation provides little direct support. Missing means no original item; new omissions live in the checklist/gap register. No original scope string becomes Excellent simply because it contains many concepts.

The files are complementary representations of one supplied design, not three independent endorsements. Master/catalog repeated modules/topics are true representational duplicates. Four repeated topic titles are intentional revisits: corporate actions M03.12/M54.06 (lifecycle then data adjustments), auctions M04.12/M50.10 (order literacy then microstructure), expected value M10.06/M45.04 (probability then sizing), and reconciliation M22.15/M49.05 (assignment then operations). They are retained with their distinct depth and assessment context. No equal-title deletion is performed.

Conflicts and consequential gaps: blanket NSE Clearing cash wording is rejected for individual-stock derivatives; current operational deadlines remain bounded. R56’s PDF was recovered but inconsistent numeric headlines are quarantined. Original R60 describes file formats, so it is retained for ingestion and supplemented by the actual BSE notice. R58’s table/prose inconsistency blocks current tax calculations. The VIXMO/single-stock methodology PDFs are not substitutes for the exact VIX methodology. American-quote calibration, CAS timing changes and regulator-report arithmetic checks are explicit new topics. Chart content is moved to an optional tested specialization; it is not erased.

All 25 original gaps have individual dispositions in [gaps.json](../../research/registers/gaps.json). Missing teaching and fresh question-bank authoring are P17 exclusions; application gaps are P02 onward. Absent full-text verification and current-rule chains are bounded research exclusions. The distinction matters: a missing lesson is not repaired by adding another resource link, and a missing current circular is not repaired by a study-time estimate.
