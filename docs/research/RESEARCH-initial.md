1. **Some recent inherited references check out.** The Federal Reserve's April 17, 2026 letter explicitly replaces SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. This supports retaining the newer reference, with its banking applicability made explicit; the attachment still requires detailed reading. [Federal Reserve SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm).
2. **Resource currency needs separate events and dates.** The NSE STT page distinguishes pre-/post-April 1, 2026 provisions and tax bases. The contract directory separately links lot sizes, freezes and specifications. These support an effective-dated source register rather than facts embedded throughout lessons. This is a design inference from the sources. [NSE STT](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-securities-transaction-tax), [NSE contract information](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-information).
3. **Rule chains matter.** The initial retail algorithmic-trading circular has a later implementation notice. The two must be read with exchange standards and later amendments before describing current obligations. [February 2025 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html), [September 2025 timeline notice](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/sep-2025/extension-of-timeline-for-implementation-of-sebi-circular-dated-february-04-2025-on-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading-_96979.html).
4. **An official overview can require reconciliation.** NSE Clearing's general page contains cash-settlement wording under stock and index options, while a specific SEBI circular requires physical settlement for stock derivatives. This is a source-scope conflict to investigate through operative circulars; the overview must not be imported as a blanket current rule. [Clearing overview](https://www.nseclearing.in/clearing-settlement/equity-derivatives/settlement-mechanism), [SEBI December 2018 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/dec-2018/1546255335099.pdf).
5. **The beginner mathematics bridge has usable candidates.** MIT's calculus and linear-algebra courses expose exercises and solutions as well as lectures. They are more specific bridge candidates than sending a novice directly to graduate finance. Actual selections and prerequisites remain to be designed. [MIT calculus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/), [MIT linear algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/).
6. **Programming assignments can be tied to concrete failure cases.** Official SciPy root solvers can support IV-inversion comparisons; scikit-learn's leakage example can support a deliberately broken research workflow; arch supplies explicit conditional-volatility examples. Our financial exercises and numerical answers still need authoring. [SciPy optimization](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.html), [scikit-learn pitfalls](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html), [arch modeling](https://bashtage.github.io/arch/univariate/univariate_volatility_modeling.html).
7. **A candidate advanced gap is American-quote calibration.** The existing map recognizes American models and surface construction but does not explicitly name de-Americanization. A primary preprint reports circumstances with material approximation errors; this justifies full-text investigation and a possible competency within existing modules, not immediate adoption of a method. [Burkovska et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.06181).
8. **Historical episodes need individual evidence packs.** BIS's 2018 analysis supplies a focused starting point for volatility-product rebalancing and feedback, including a correction note. It can anchor a separate case rather than leaving 2018 grouped with several crashes in M66.15. [BIS analysis](https://www.bis.org/publications/equity-market-turbulence-5-february-role-exchange-traded-volatility-products).

## Research method for completing Phase 2

1. Review every domain against the [25-gap register](reviews/phase-1-gaps.md), distinguishing a missing concept from missing teaching, practice, evidence or maintenance.
2. Reverify each inherited resource's identity, edition, accessible scope and purpose. Preserve failed/blocked checks and author/publisher alternatives. Do not imply full-text review from a title match.
3. Select exact readings, sections and lectures, with beginner alternatives and explicit prerequisites. Record free/paid/access conditions and estimated assigned study time as separate fields.
4. Read priority primary papers and exact methodologies. For empirical claims, record population, period, assumptions, uncertainty, costs, limitations and competing evidence. For mathematical claims, state contract/model assumptions and numerical conventions.
5. Check rules by jurisdiction, product, issuer, publication date, effective date and amendment chain. Store source conflicts and resolution evidence. Distinguish enacted, proposed, historical and broker-specific provisions.
6. Define original exercises that discriminate understanding: unfamiliar numbers, edge cases, operational cash flows, falsification, reproducibility and counterexamples. Use licensed or synthetic data and independently check answer keys.
7. Assign each gap a supported disposition and target competency. Repeat the professional-role review before freezing the canonical curriculum.

The phase is complete only when all domains and gaps have an evidence-backed disposition, priority sources have sufficient content review, and unresolved issues are bounded and visibly deferred. Resource quantity alone is not an exit condition.
