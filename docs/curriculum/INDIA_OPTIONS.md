# India and global rules — dated research, not permanent constants

Verification date:22 September 2026. The machine register is [market_rules.json](../../data/v1/market_rules.json). Historical notices establish historical transitions; they do not certify every currently listed series. Each worksheet must identify exchange, product, underlying, expiry/contract version, multiplier, exercise style, cash/physical settlement, currency, fees, taxes, margin, restrictions, publication/effective dates and broker procedures.

The settlement-source conflict is resolved at the principle level: the generic NSE Clearing overview’s blanket cash-exercise wording must not be used for individual-stock derivatives. [SEBI2018/161](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/dec-2018/1546255335099.pdf) mandated phased physical settlement of stock derivatives, and [NCL/CMPT/73864](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMPT73864.pdf) supplies a 2026 instrument-specific physical-settlement example. Index and stock rules must be separate. Current delivery deadlines, netting, shortages, collateral and broker cutoffs still require the operative consolidated chain and exact contract.

R56 was retrieved and examined beyond its landing page. The SEBI FY25–FY26 report uses top 15-broker data and a separate 5050-trader clearing sample; peak margin is not a complete return-on-capital denominator. Its Table 5 shows −5430 lakh for 105 major sellers while nearby prose says 543 crore; the average column is per person while another sentence refers to loss-making persons. These incompatible units/denominators are **quarantined**, not silently corrected. Use [the official report](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2026/1787233601328.pdf), pp 10–19, as an evidence-audit case; do not publish the disputed buyer/seller loss headline. Authority does not eliminate the need to reconcile arithmetic.

The first India practice worksheet therefore uses a fully specified hypothetical contract and separately asks learners to locate a dated real contract. A missing master/circular is a valid “cannot verify” answer. The curriculum never substitutes memory for a current lot, expiry, margin or charge.

Maintenance: every India rule gets a seven-day editorial review interval, plus event-triggered review on a new notice, contract-master change, contradiction or user correction. Other market rules default to30 days. These intervals are editorial choices, not a guarantee that rules remain valid during the interval. The validator’s explicit `--as-of` flags aged records; unknown effective dates and incomplete supersession chains block current operational publication immediately. Superseded records remain archival, linked to successors. An editor must compare the operative notice, effective window and contract master, then document the decision and affected lessons/assessments. Broker cutoffs and account-specific permissions are separately checked at use.

Global comparison uses US OCC/FINRA, CME futures options, HKEX, Eurex and EU/UK frameworks to teach how conventions differ. It does not certify all current local legal obligations. US portfolio-margin thresholds and EU/UK detailed requirements remain excluded until their complete current chains are reviewed.
