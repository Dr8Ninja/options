# M56 — Backtesting, validation and simulation

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `backtesting-validation-and-simulation`. Intermediate; 16–40 hours. Tags: quantitative, research-data-and-software.

Simulate the complete option lifecycle with fills, exercise, capital and chronological validation.

Hard prerequisites: C-M55, C-M34, C-M47, C-M58. Recommended preparation: C-M10, C-M52. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can correct payoff arithmetic still create a fraudulent-looking backtest? Bridge: B03.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Simulate the complete option lifecycle with fills, exercise, capital and chronological validation.
- Solve and explain: Gross dailyedge 5 lessspread 3 fee 1 funding 2 equals-1; reject frictionless profitability.
- Audit the failure mode: Look-ahead fills, missing assignment and unlimited capital.

Mathematics: S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: B03 plus arrays, functions and tests.

Reading: R23:selection risk; R51:data overview; R58:effective-dated costs. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M56.01 — Backtest architectures: vectorized, event-driven and hybrid; appropriate use
- M56.02 — Options contract selection: contemporaneous chain, strike/expiry availability and liquidity
- M56.03 — Executable fills: bid/ask, package fills, delays, partial fills and adverse selection
- M56.04 — Full cost model: commissions, taxes, spread, impact, funding and borrow
- M56.05 — Lifecycle simulation: exercise, assignment, dividends, corporate actions and settlement
- M56.06 — Capital accounting: premium cash, mark-to-market, margin, collateral and forced liquidation
- M56.07 — In-sample/out-of-sample: separation, final holdout and contamination controls
- M56.08 — Walk-forward testing: expanding/rolling windows, retraining and parameter selection
- M56.09 — Time-aware cross-validation: purge overlapping labels, embargo where needed, no random leakage
- M56.10 — Parameter optimization: nested validation, stability and search-budget accounting
- M56.11 — Monte Carlo strategy simulation: paths, regime assumptions and distribution uncertainty
- M56.12 — Trade/return bootstrap: block dependence, overlapping positions and sequence risk
- M56.13 — Forward testing: live data, paper orders, logged decisions and matching execution assumptions
- M56.14 — Paper-trading limitations: optimistic fills, absent impact, partial assignment modeling and behavior
- M56.15 — Promotion criteria: validated edge, limits, reconciliation, stress and stop conditions
- M56.16 — Model degradation: forecast drift, execution drift, structural breaks and retirement rules

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Look-ahead fills, missing assignment and unlimited capital.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- Why can correct payoff arithmetic still create a fraudulent-looking backtest?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?

Mastery:

- Reproduce a hand-calculated sample and preserve a genuinely untouched holdout.
- At least 85% on quiz and rubric; no unresolved critical errors; solve a fresh transfer variant with reasoning.

Remediation: Diagnose the mistaken unit, model, observation or cash-flow step; revisit the cited entry bridge or source section, annotate the correction, then attempt an unseen variant after a separate review session.
