# M29 — No-arbitrage, carry and valuation foundations

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `no-arbitrage-carry-and-valuation-foundations`. Intermediate; 15–38 hours. Tags: options-pricing, pricing-greeks-and-hedging.

Derive bounds and replication with explicit carry and exercise assumptions.

Hard prerequisites: C-M24, C-B02. Recommended preparation: C-M05, C-M10, C-M21. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: What assumption fails if a parity trade needs unavailable short stock? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Derive bounds and replication with explicit carry and exercise assumptions.
- Solve and explain: S100 K100 r 5% q 0 T1: C-P=4.87705755 under European continuous-rate assumptions.
- Audit the failure mode: Using European parity as equality for American contracts.

Mathematics: A, C (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: RX02:assumptions; R05:11. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M29.01 — No-arbitrage logic: dominance, replication, law of one price and assumptions
- M29.02 — Forward pricing: spot, discount factors, dividends, borrow and carry
- M29.03 — Futures versus forwards: daily settlement and interest-rate covariance
- M29.04 — Put-call parity: European exercise, matched terms, discrete/continuous distributions
- M29.05 — American parity bounds: exercise rights and financing inequalities
- M29.06 — Price bounds: positivity, intrinsic/exercise relations and strike monotonicity
- M29.07 — Convexity in strike: butterfly bounds and probability-density implications
- M29.08 — Calendar consistency: dividends, carry and correct normalization before comparing maturities
- M29.09 — Replication and hedging: one-period stock/bond portfolios and state-contingent cash flows
- M29.10 — Risk-neutral valuation: pricing measure versus belief about realized returns
- M29.11 — Discount curves: collateral, funding, OIS and multi-curve relevance
- M29.12 — Implied forwards: option parity, noisy quotes and dividend/borrow estimates
- M29.13 — Incomplete markets: multiple pricing measures, unhedgeable risks and risk premia
- M29.14 — Model price versus market value: executable quotes, reserve, bid/ask and uncertainty
- M29.15 — Exercise value versus European price bounds: carry and delayed exercisability; why immediate intrinsic-value shortcuts can mislead
