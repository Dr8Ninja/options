# M27 — Time, ratio and asymmetric structures

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `time-ratio-and-asymmetric-structures`. Advanced; 32–60 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Analyze calendars, diagonals, ratios and backspreads on a multi-date path.

Hard prerequisites: C-M25, C-M32. Recommended preparation: C-M26. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why is a calendar's final P&L not fixed by today's payoff diagram? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Analyze calendars, diagonals, ratios and backspreads on a multi-date path.
- Solve and explain: Sell one 100 call buy two 110 calls for net credit 1: expiry 115 gives -15+10+1=-4; at 130 gives 11.
- Audit the failure mode: Claiming a model-free calendar maximum profit or ignoring ratio tail exposure.

Mathematics: A, C (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R05:12 TOC-based; RX01:assignment. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M27.01 — Long calendar spread: front/back expiry, term structure and front-expiry valuation
- M27.02 — Short calendar spread: reverse maturity exposure and margin behavior
- M27.03 — Diagonal spread: strike and maturity differences; directional plus surface exposure
- M27.04 — Double calendar: two strikes, event placement and changing payoff profile
- M27.05 — Double diagonal: two diagonals, skew and expiry interaction
- M27.06 — Poor man's covered call: long-call diagonal, exercise funding and stock-cover distinction
- M27.07 — Call ratio spread: unmatched short calls, upside tail and net Greek changes
- M27.08 — Put ratio spread: unmatched short puts, downside concentration and funding
- M27.09 — Call backspread: more long calls than short, valley loss and upside convexity
- M27.10 — Put backspread: downside convexity, intermediate loss zone and skew cost
- M27.11 — Ratio butterfly: unequal contract weights, extra tail exposure and exact construction
- M27.12 — Christmas tree spread: unequal strike spacing, weights and convention differences
- M27.13 — Jade lizard: short put plus call credit spread; construction-dependent upper-side outcome
- M27.14 — Reverse jade lizard: short call plus put credit spread; downside/upside asymmetry
- M27.15 — Maturity-mismatch risk: no single universal expiry payoff or model-free maximum profit
- M27.16 — Structure naming ambiguity: verify signed legs, strikes, expiries and multipliers before analysis
