# M26 — Straddles, strangles, butterflies and condors

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `straddles-strangles-butterflies-and-condors`. Intermediate; 15–38 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Compare volatility and convexity exposures of straddles, strangles and butterflies/condors.

Hard prerequisites: C-M25, C-M10. Recommended preparation: none. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can a high win-rate condor have negative expectancy? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Compare volatility and convexity exposures of straddles, strangles and butterflies/condors.
- Solve and explain: 100 straddle costs 8: break-evens 92/108 before costs; 10-point move yields 2.
- Audit the failure mode: Equating terminal range width with edge or safety.

Mathematics: A, C (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R06:strategy reference TOC only; R01:X. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M26.01 — Long straddle: two-sided convexity, premium, movement and volatility sensitivity
- M26.02 — Short straddle: concentrated short gamma, tails, margin and assignment
- M26.03 — Long strangle: cheaper premium, farther strikes and larger required movement
- M26.04 — Short strangle: high win-rate appearance, gap losses and skew exposure
- M26.05 — Long call butterfly: strike weights, tent payoff and local exposure
- M26.06 — Long put butterfly: construction, call equivalence and exercise differences
- M26.07 — Short butterfly: reverse exposure, debit/credit conventions and tails
- M26.08 — Iron butterfly: credit and reverse variants, body concentration and expiry effects
- M26.09 — Broken-wing butterfly: asymmetric wings, credit/debit and residual side risk
- M26.10 — Long call/put condor: four strikes, plateau payoff and wing widths
- M26.11 — Short condor: inverse terminal shape and financing
- M26.12 — Iron condor: short-volatility structure, correlated wings and assignment
- M26.13 — Reverse iron condor: long movement, bounded profit and premium risk
- M26.14 — Iron versus single-option-type structures: parity, cost, margin and operational differences
- M26.15 — Wing selection: skew cost, expected shortfall, liquidity and realistic exit prices
