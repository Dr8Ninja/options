# M53 — Position management and decision policies

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `position-management-and-decision-policies`. Intermediate; 17–42 hours. Tags: position-management, microstructure-execution-and-management.

Precommit trade adjustment and exit rules based on thesis, risk and costs.

Hard prerequisites: C-M25, C-M45, C-M52. Recommended preparation: C-M22, C-M24, C-M32. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Does rolling reset the economic loss of a position? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Precommit trade adjustment and exit rules based on thesis, risk and costs.
- Solve and explain: Close atloss 200 then open credit 50: historical loss remains 200;50 creates a new liability.
- Audit the failure mode: Chasing recovery, confusing credits with profit.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:X; RX01:exercise risk. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M53.01 — Management baseline: hold/close/adjust comparison from current exposure, not sunk cost
- M53.02 — Scaling in: preplanned increments, changing aggregate risk and liquidity
- M53.03 — Scaling out: risk reduction, lost convexity, costs and minimum size
- M53.04 — Rolling up/down: closing/reopening, realized P&L and changed exposure
- M53.05 — Rolling out: term risk, events, funding and new trade evaluation
- M53.06 — Rolling for credit: cash credit versus profit and added obligations
- M53.07 — Defensive adjustments: thesis validity, incremental expected value and total loss budget
- M53.08 — Greek adjustments: reducing delta/gamma, adding/removing vega and managing theta tradeoffs
- M53.09 — Position conversion: changed structure, basis, liquidity and operational risk
- M53.10 — Premium-based stops: spread distortions, stale marks and volatility sensitivity
- M53.11 — Underlying-based stops: conditional option loss, gaps and execution delay
- M53.12 — Volatility-based exits: forecast invalidation, surface movement and model dependence
- M53.13 — Time-based exits: DTE, events, decay, gamma and opportunity cost
- M53.14 — Profit targets: expectancy, truncating winners and dependence on strategy distribution
- M53.15 — Expiration management: assignment, exercise, pinning and settlement cash needs
- M53.16 — Adjustment backtests: complete policy simulation, costs and counterfactual comparison
- M53.17 — Exit contingency: halts, lost connectivity, partial fills and unavailable hedges

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Chasing recovery, confusing credits with profit.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- Does rolling reset the economic loss of a position?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?
