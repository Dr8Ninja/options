# M03 — Institutions, venues and the trade lifecycle

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `institutions-venues-and-the-trade-lifecycle`. Beginner; 14–36 hours. Tags: market-mechanics, financial-and-market-foundations.

Trace a trade from broker through clearing to final cash or delivery.

Hard prerequisites: C-M21. Recommended preparation: C-M01, C-M02. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Who owes what after assignment, and who guarantees whose performance? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Trace a trade from broker through clearing to final cash or delivery.
- Solve and explain: A physical call with K=100 and multiplier 100 requires 10000 cash on exercise and receives 100 shares, separate from premium.
- Audit the failure mode: Confusing exercise style with settlement medium.

Mathematics: A (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:III and VIII; R04:exercise and assignment. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M03.01 — Market participants: investors, hedgers, speculators, market makers, arbitrageurs, asset managers
- M03.02 — Broker roles: agency, principal, introducing broker, clearing broker, prime broker
- M03.03 — Exchange roles: listing, matching, surveillance, rulebooks, trading calendars
- M03.04 — Clearing houses: novation, netting, central counterparties, default management
- M03.05 — Custody and depositories: beneficial ownership, segregation, reconciliation, asset protection limits
- M03.06 — Trade lifecycle: order, acknowledgment, match, execution report, clearing, settlement
- M03.07 — Settlement conventions: trade date, value date, cash and securities delivery, holidays
- M03.08 — Delivery versus payment: settlement risk, fails, buy-ins, settlement finality
- M03.09 — CCP default waterfall: margin, default fund, mutualization, residual counterparty exposure
- M03.10 — Trading sessions: regular, overnight, pre-market, after-hours; product-specific availability
- M03.11 — Halts and circuit breakers: market-wide halts, price bands, limit states, reopening auctions
- M03.12 — Corporate actions: splits, dividends, rights, spin-offs, mergers, delistings
- M03.13 — Reference data: instrument identifiers, symbology, currency, contract version and calendar
- M03.14 — Broker selection: regulatory status, execution, stability, custody, support and exercise procedures
