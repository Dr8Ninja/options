# M51 — Options market making and dealer-flow analysis

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `options-market-making-and-dealer-flow-analysis`. Advanced; 30–57 hours. Tags: microstructure, microstructure-execution-and-management.

Balance spread revenue, hedging costs, inventory and toxic flow in quoting models.

Hard prerequisites: C-M50, C-M34, C-M47. Recommended preparation: C-M37. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why is dealer gamma from open interest unidentified without position assumptions? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Balance spread revenue, hedging costs, inventory and toxic flow in quoting models.
- Solve and explain: OI100, gamma.02, multiplier 100 gives absolute 200 share-delta/unit spot if all same sign; signednet can differ drastically.
- Audit the failure mode: Assigning every customer trade to one dealer sign.

Mathematics: C, S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R49:author paper listing; R32:positioning study caveats. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

- M51.05 — Dealer gamma narratives: conditional hedge direction, inventory sign and unknown positions
- M51.06 — Gamma exposure maps: calculation, multiplier, dollar conventions and sign assumptions
- M51.07 — Vanna flows: spot-vol co-movement, inventory and hedge-policy assumptions
- M51.08 — Charm flows: passage of time, changing delta and offsetting positions
- M51.09 — Expiration effects: rolls, exercise, hedge unwinds and settlement-related flows
- M51.10 — Pinning: possible mechanisms, conditional evidence and failure cases
- M51.11 — 0DTE market impact: customer/dealer netting, gross volume versus net risk
- M51.12 — Trade-sign inference: complex packages, stock hedges and missing OTC exposures
- M51.13 — Hedge feedback loops: stabilizing/destabilizing possibilities and market conditions
