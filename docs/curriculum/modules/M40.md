# M40 — Advanced volatility models and calibration

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `advanced-volatility-models-and-calibration`. Advanced; 28–54 hours. Tags: quantitative, volatility-and-volatility-trading.

Compare local/stochastic/jump models by calibration fit, identifiability and hedge error.

Hard prerequisites: C-M37, C-M14. Recommended preparation: C-M31. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why does fitting vanillas not identify barrier risk uniquely? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Compare local/stochastic/jump models by calibration fit, identifiability and hedge error.
- Solve and explain: Two models both price call 10 yet price barrier 2 and 3: mark model spread 1; no observed truth follows.
- Audit the failure mode: Choosing complexity by in-sample fit; invalid Heston/SABR parameter regions.

Mathematics: C, L, N, I (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: B03 plus arrays, functions and tests.

Reading: R39/R40:full text access pending; R35:surface baseline. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M40.01 — Local volatility: Dupire equation, input surface, density and dynamics limitations
- M40.02 — Stochastic volatility: latent variance, leverage correlation and volatility risk premium
- M40.03 — Heston model: mean-reverting variance, characteristic function, calibration and numerical issues
- M40.04 — SABR: alpha, beta, rho, nu, asymptotics and normal/lognormal conventions
- M40.05 — Jump diffusion: Merton model, jump distribution and incomplete hedging
- M40.06 — Bates and stochastic-local volatility: combined risks, calibration and complexity
- M40.07 — SVI: smile parameterization, total variance and static-arbitrage constraints
- M40.08 — SSVI and eSSVI: surface parameterization, cross-maturity consistency and calibration
- M40.09 — Rough volatility: empirical motivation, rough Bergomi/Heston, simulation and research limitations
- M40.10 — Local-stochastic/jump alternatives: model selection for the actual product and hedge objective
- M40.11 — Calibration identification: non-unique parameters, regularization and stability
- M40.12 — Model validation: fit, hedging performance, stress, benchmark and extrapolation tests
- M40.13 — Risk-neutral versus physical dynamics: estimation versus calibration and risk premiums
- M40.14 — Model risk reserves: uncertainty bands, challenger models and governance

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.
