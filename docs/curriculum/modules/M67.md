# M67 — Professional desk practice and ongoing development

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `professional-desk-practice-and-ongoing-development`. Advanced; 30–57 hours. Tags: professional-practice, evidence-failure-analysis-and-professional-practice.

Integrate research, risk, execution and operations in an independently reviewable simulated desk.

Hard prerequisites: C-M56, C-M48, C-M49, C-M52, C-M61. Recommended preparation: C-M51, C-M58, C-M66. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Would another person reproduce the same decision using the record available then? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Integrate research, risk, execution and operations in an independently reviewable simulated desk.
- Solve and explain: Reconcile openingequity 10000 plusnetP&L-120 andcashdeposit 500 toclosing 10380.
- Audit the failure mode: Equating real-money profits with competence or omitting incident review.

Mathematics: A, S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: B03 plus arrays, functions and tests.

Reading: R45/R48:governance and continuity; canonical capstone C07. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M67.01 — Desk mandate: permitted markets, horizons, risk budgets, capital, capacity and objectives
- M67.02 — Daily risk meeting: overnight P&L, events, stress, liquidity and planned exposure
- M67.03 — Front/middle/back office: trading, independent risk, confirmations, clearing and settlement
- M67.04 — P&L ownership: trade capture, independent prices, reserves and unexplained residuals
- M67.05 — Risk limits: Greek, scenario, concentration, liquidity, counterparty and drawdown constraints
- M67.06 — Limit breaches: escalation, authority, remediation, documentation and prevention
- M67.07 — Model inventory: owner, version, purpose, assumptions, validation and retirement
- M67.08 — Research-to-production: review, shadow operation, change control and ongoing monitoring
- M67.09 — Capital and funding desk: collateral, financing, borrow, liquidity forecasts and allocation
- M67.10 — Compliance culture: conflicts, restricted information, surveillance and record retention
- M67.11 — Communication: concise trade thesis, quantified risk, uncertainty and dissent
- M67.12 — Capacity and business economics: data, infrastructure, staffing, return on capital and scalability
- M67.13 — Mentorship and review: observed practice, independent challenge and correction of blind spots
- M67.14 — Continuing competence: rule changes, new products, literature, incident learning and requalification
- M67.15 — Research frontier: new models and execution methods; relevance determined by measurable benefit

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Practice:

**EX-M67-C** Would another person reproduce the same decision using the record available then?

Reference: Use the module objective and stated failure mode; credit explicit conditions and a counterexample, not confidence.

**EX-M67-N** Compute, show units and assumptions; then change one input and justify the direction. Reconcile openingequity 10000 plusnetP&L-120 andcashdeposit 500 toclosing 10380.

Reference: Reconcile openingequity 10000 plusnetP&L-120 andcashdeposit 500 toclosing 10380.

**EX-M67-P** Present a desk-style proposal, daily risk report and incident response to a skeptical reviewer.

Reference: Defend assumptions, admit unknowns, reconcile numbers and comply with a written mandate.

Common mistakes:

- Equating real-money profits with competence or omitting incident review.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- Would another person reproduce the same decision using the record available then?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?

Mastery:

- Defend assumptions, admit unknowns, reconcile numbers and comply with a written mandate.
- At least 85% on quiz and rubric; no unresolved critical errors; solve a fresh transfer variant with reasoning.

Remediation: Diagnose the mistaken unit, model, observation or cash-flow step; revisit the cited entry bridge or source section, annotate the correction, then attempt an unseen variant after a separate review session.
