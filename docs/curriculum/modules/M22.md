# M22 — Exercise, assignment and expiration operations

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `exercise-assignment-and-expiration-operations`. Beginner; 15–38 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Reconstruct exercise, assignment, dividend and expiration cash paths.

Hard prerequisites: C-M03. Recommended preparation: C-M21, C-M04, C-M05. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can a spread temporarily create a large stock or cash obligation? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Reconstruct exercise, assignment, dividend and expiration cash paths.
- Solve and explain: Short one 100-strike put premium 2 multiplier 100 assigned: pay 10000, receive 100 shares; effective acquisition 98 before costs.
- Audit the failure mode: Assuming the long leg is automatically exercised to rescue a short leg.

Mathematics: A (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:VIII and X; RX01:full article. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M22.01 — Voluntary exercise: instruction process, deadlines and loss of remaining extrinsic value
- M22.02 — Exercise by exception: thresholds, broker policies, contrary instructions and exceptions
- M22.03 — Assignment mechanics: clearing allocation, broker allocation, long-holder versus short-writer choices
- M22.04 — Early assignment: American exercise, dividends, rates, borrow and residual time value
- M22.05 — Call early exercise: dividend economics, financing, remaining optionality and assumptions
- M22.06 — Put early exercise: interest benefit, deep ITM positions and optionality tradeoff
- M22.07 — Ex-dividend risk: dates, cash flows, short stock obligations and exercise decisions
- M22.08 — Pin risk: uncertain assignment near strike and post-close price changes
- M22.09 — Expiration mismatch: one leg exercises, the other does not; unexpected stock or futures
- M22.10 — AM versus PM settlement: settlement sampling, last trade and overnight basis risk
- M22.11 — Cash settlement value: special quotation, closing methodology and settlement prints
- M22.12 — Physical delivery: cash/shares required, delivery margin, shortages and forced liquidation
- M22.13 — Corporate action adjustments: OCC/exchange notices, mergers, special dividends and accelerated expiries
- M22.14 — Expiration decision tree: close, roll, exercise, lapse; operational deadlines and contingency plan
- M22.15 — Position reconciliation: resulting stock/futures, cash ledger, fees and next-session risk

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Assuming the long leg is automatically exercised to rescue a short leg.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
