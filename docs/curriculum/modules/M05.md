# M05 — Trading costs, financing and basic margin

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `trading-costs-financing-and-basic-margin`. Beginner; 14–36 hours. Tags: risk, financial-and-market-foundations.

Calculate executable net economics including funding, spread, fees and tax basis.

Hard prerequisites: C-M04. Recommended preparation: C-M01, C-M03. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why is broker margin neither a stop nor a maximum loss? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Calculate executable net economics including funding, spread, fees and tax basis.
- Solve and explain: Gross profit 80 less fees 12, crossing 20 and funding 8 gives 40 net; double crossing leaves 20.
- Audit the failure mode: Ignoring exercise charges or using present tax rates in old samples.

Mathematics: A (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:IX; R58:dated STT table only. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M05.01 — Explicit costs: commissions, exchange, clearing, regulatory fees and taxes
- M05.02 — Implicit costs: bid/ask spread, slippage, price impact, opportunity cost
- M05.03 — Funding costs: debit interest, credit interest, borrow fees, collateral opportunity cost
- M05.04 — Cash and margin accounts: permitted activity, settled funds, broker approval levels
- M05.05 — Initial and maintenance margin: collateral requirements versus economic risk
- M05.06 — Buying power: available margin, reserved cash, position offsets, house add-ons
- M05.07 — Margin is not maximum loss: stress losses, liquidation, deficits and recourse
- M05.08 — Short selling: locate, borrow recall, buy-ins, dividends owed, squeeze risk
- M05.09 — Cash-secured exposure: reserved cash, interest treatment, collateral haircuts
- M05.10 — Mark-to-market cash flows: daily/intraday variation margin, premium payment conventions
- M05.11 — Cost-aware expectancy: round trips, repeated adjustments, small-premium cost burden
- M05.12 — Turnover and capacity: trade frequency, size, liquidity and net returns
- M05.13 — Taxes versus trading edge: pre-tax and after-tax evaluation; timing and instrument dependence
- M05.14 — Funding asymmetry: borrowing/lending spreads, margin netting sets, balance-sheet constraints
