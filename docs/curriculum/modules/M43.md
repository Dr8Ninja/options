# M43 — Rates, FX, commodities, credit and digital-asset options

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `rates-fx-commodities-credit-and-digital-asset-options`. Advanced; 30–57 hours. Tags: cross-asset-derivatives, futures-and-cross-asset-derivatives.

Handle rates, FX and commodities with product-specific numeraires and conventions.

Hard prerequisites: C-M42, C-M30. Recommended preparation: C-M07. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why is equity BSM with zero yield wrong for an FX option? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Objectives:

- Handle rates, FX and commodities with product-specific numeraires and conventions.
- Solve and explain: FX S1.1 domestic 5% foreign 3% T1 gives forward 1.12222147.
- Audit the failure mode: Ignoring foreign rates, commodity storage or negative-rate model limits.

- M43.09 — Commodity options: seasonality, inventory, convenience yield and spread optionality
- M43.10 — Spread options: calendar, crack, spark and location spreads; correlation risk
- M43.11 — Commodity operational optionality: storage, swing and delivery flexibility
- M43.12 — Credit derivatives: CDS, recovery, index tranches and default correlation
- M43.13 — Convertible bonds: equity optionality, credit spread, dilution and borrow
- M43.14 — Digital-asset options: collateral denomination, inverse/linear payoff and perpetual basis
