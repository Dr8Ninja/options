# M32 — Primary Greeks and exposure units

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `primary-greeks-and-exposure-units`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: options-greeks, pricing-greeks-and-hedging.

Translate primary Greeks into cash exposures and verify signs and finite differences.

Hard prerequisites: C-M30. Recommended preparation: none. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why must vega units state decimal volatility or one volatility point? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Translate primary Greeks into cash exposures and verify signs and finite differences.
- Solve and explain: Delta .5 gamma .02 shock 2 predicts 1.04 price change; vega 20 per unit times .01 gives .20.
- Audit the failure mode: Mixing per-share, per-contract and cash Greeks.

Mathematics: C, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R05:19; RX03:BSM. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M32.01 — Delta: spot sensitivity, call/put signs, hedge ratio and model dependence
- M32.02 — Gamma: second spot derivative, convexity and changing delta; not a first-order Greek
- M32.03 — Theta: passage-of-time convention, calendar/trading days and sign exceptions
- M32.04 — Vega: volatility sensitivity, one volatility point versus unit volatility
- M32.05 — Rho: rate sensitivity, maturity, financing and exercise effects
- M32.06 — Greek units: per unit, contract, currency, percent move and total position
- M32.07 — Position Greeks: signed quantities, multipliers, currencies and aggregation
- M32.08 — Greek profiles across spot: ATM concentration, wings and nonlinear transitions
- M32.09 — Greek profiles across time: near-expiry gamma, theta, vega and singular limits
- M32.10 — Greek profiles across volatility: curvature, changing moneyness and model choice
- M32.11 — Delta conventions: spot, forward, premium-adjusted and sticky-surface assumptions
- M32.12 — Delta versus probability: N(d1), N(d2), pricing measure and real-world distinctions
- M32.13 — Elasticity: lambda/omega, percent sensitivity and instability near zero option value
- M32.14 — Portfolio risk normalization: dollar delta, cash gamma, vega buckets and beta adjustment

Common mistakes:

- Mixing per-share, per-contract and cash Greeks.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
