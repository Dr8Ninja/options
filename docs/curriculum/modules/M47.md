# M47 — Margin, collateral and liquidity management

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `margin-collateral-and-liquidity-management`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: risk, risk-and-portfolio-construction.

Stress collateral, funding and margin alongside economic risk.

Hard prerequisites: C-M05, C-M22. Recommended preparation: C-M45. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can hedges reduce delta but increase cash needs? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Stress collateral, funding and margin alongside economic risk.
- Solve and explain: Cash 1000, initialmargin 600 and stressincrease 500: shortfall 100 before liquidationcost.
- Audit the failure mode: Assuming portfolio-margin offsets persist or NSE SPAN equals CME SPAN2.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R09:framework formula; R61:(g). Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M47.01 — Strategy-based margin: permitted offsets, uncovered options and broker house rules
- M47.02 — Portfolio margin: risk arrays, scenario offsets, eligibility and minimums
- M47.03 — SPAN and SPAN 2: methodology differences, product coverage, stress/liquidity/concentration components
- M47.04 — Other clearing frameworks: Eurex Prisma, local clearing models and documentation
- M47.05 — Funding versus market liquidity: ability to meet cash calls versus ability to trade
- M47.06 — Liquidity reserves: premium, variation margin, assignment, fees and emergency cash
- M47.07 — Procyclicality: volatility-driven margin increases and liquidation feedback
- M47.08 — Collateral quality: haircuts, concentration, eligibility, currency mismatch and settlement timing
- M47.09 — Netting limitations: legal entities, accounts, brokers, CCPs and product boundaries
- M47.10 — Financing locked trades: mark-to-market stress despite eventual contractual convergence
- M47.11 — Borrow availability stress: recalls, higher fees, buy-ins and hedge substitution
- M47.12 — Broker liquidation procedures: order priority, forced closures and notification limits
- M47.13 — Cash-flow ladder: daily obligations, settlement dates, stress calls and funding buffers
- M47.14 — Liquidity-adjusted sizing: exit depth, days-to-liquidate, slippage and crowded exposures
