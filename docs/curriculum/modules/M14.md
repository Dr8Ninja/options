# M14 — Stochastic processes and mathematical finance

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `stochastic-processes-and-mathematical-finance`. Advanced; 28–54 hours. Tags: mathematics, mathematics-and-inference.

Explain Brownian scaling, Itô correction and the risk-neutral measure under stated assumptions.

Hard prerequisites: C-M13, C-M10. Recommended preparation: C-M12. Optional enrichment: C-M40. Diagnostic: Why is a pricing measure not a forecast of actual returns? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Explain Brownian scaling, Itô correction and the risk-neutral measure under stated assumptions.
- Solve and explain: If dS/S=mu dt+sigma dW, dlogS drift is mu-sigma²/2; mu=.08 sigma=.2 gives .06.
- Audit the failure mode: Treating every process as continuous or every market as complete.

Mathematics: C, S, I (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R24:Itô and risk-neutral valuation, exact pages pending licensed access. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M14.01 — Measure-theoretic probability: sigma-algebras, filtrations, measurability and conditional expectation
- M14.02 — Brownian motion: increments, scaling, quadratic variation and simulation
- M14.03 — Martingales: fair-game structure, optional stopping conditions and pricing relevance
- M14.04 — Stochastic integration: Ito integrals, adapted processes and integrability
- M14.05 — Ito lemma: first/second-order terms and multidimensional versions
- M14.06 — Stochastic differential equations: existence, discretization, weak/strong convergence
- M14.07 — Geometric Brownian motion: log returns, drift, diffusion and positivity assumptions
- M14.08 — Change of measure: Radon–Nikodym derivatives, Girsanov, physical versus pricing dynamics
- M14.09 — Fundamental asset-pricing theorems: no arbitrage, equivalent martingale measures, completeness
- M14.10 — Numeraires: money-market, forward and stock measures; pricing consistency
- M14.11 — Feynman–Kac: PDE and conditional-expectation representations
- M14.12 — Jump processes: Poisson, compound Poisson, Levy processes and compensators
- M14.13 — Optimal stopping: Snell envelope, American exercise and free boundaries
- M14.14 — Stochastic control: Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equations and inventory optimization
