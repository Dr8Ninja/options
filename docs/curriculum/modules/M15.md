Ordered scope:

- M15.01 — OHLC and bars: opens, highs, lows, closes, aggregation and session boundaries
- M15.02 — Candlestick anatomy: body, upper/lower shadows, range, gaps and close location
- M15.03 — Chart scales: linear, logarithmic, adjusted/unadjusted and total-return charts
- M15.04 — Alternative charts: line, tick, volume, range, Renko, Kagi, point-and-figure, Heikin-Ashi
- M15.05 — Swing structure: swing highs/lows, higher highs/lows, lower highs/lows
- M15.06 — Trend and range: directional persistence, rotation, transition and ambiguity
- M15.07 — Support and resistance: zones, prior extrema, repeated tests, role reversal
- M15.08 — Supply and demand zones: operational definitions and non-observable intent
- M15.09 — Consolidation: compression, balance, volatility contraction and expansion
- M15.10 — Breakout and breakdown: close-based versus intrabar definitions and confirmation
- M15.11 — Failed breakouts: rejection, failed follow-through, trapping narratives as hypotheses
- M15.12 — Gaps: common, breakaway, continuation/runaway, exhaustion and ex-dividend gaps
- M15.13 — Momentum and exhaustion: acceleration, deceleration, trend continuation and reversal
- M15.14 — Multi-timeframe analysis: nested horizons, inconsistent signals and avoiding hindsight
- M15.15 — Market context: location, session, liquidity, events, volatility regime and trend age
- M15.16 — Liquidity sweeps and stop runs: observed price/volume versus inferred intent
