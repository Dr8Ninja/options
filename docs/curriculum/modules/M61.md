# M61 — Journaling, performance measurement and attribution

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `journaling-performance-measurement-and-attribution`. Intermediate; 19–46 hours. Tags: performance-analytics, decision-practice-and-professional-development.

Maintain a journal connecting forecast, exposure, execution and attribution.

Hard prerequisites: C-M11, C-M60. Recommended preparation: C-M45, C-M52, C-M53. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why is Sharpe unstable for short histories with rare losses? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Maintain a journal connecting forecast, exposure, execution and attribution.
- Solve and explain: Returns 1,1,1,-5 sum-2 before compounding; three wins conceal negative total.
- Audit the failure mode: Deleting losers or mixing process ratings with P&L.

Mathematics: A, S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R53:selection-adjusted performance; R66:metadata only. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M61.01 — Trade identity: instrument, contract terms, timestamp, side, quantity and linked legs
- M61.02 — Decision record: setup, thesis, regime, confirmation, invalidation and alternatives
- M61.03 — Risk record: planned size, maximum/stress loss, margin, capital and portfolio contribution
- M61.04 — Market record: spot, bid/ask, IV, Greeks, surface, events and timestamped screenshots
- M61.05 — Behavior record: confidence, emotions, rule adherence and override rationale
- M61.06 — Execution record: orders, fills, partial fills, slippage, fees and missed trades
- M61.07 — Outcome record: cash flows, net P&L, capital employed, assignment and adjustments
- M61.08 — MFE and MAE: path excursions, executable marks and alignment with stop/target policy
- M61.09 — R-multiples: initial risk definition, changing size and limitations for undefined-risk positions
- M61.10 — Core statistics: win rate, average win/loss, expectancy, payoff ratio and profit factor
- M61.11 — Drawdown analysis: depth, duration, recovery, clustering and capital withdrawals
- M61.12 — Risk-adjusted metrics: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, information ratio and benchmark choices
- M61.13 — Return distributions: skew, kurtosis, tail losses, autocorrelation and smoothing
- M61.14 — Performance slicing: setup, regime, time of day, DTE, delta, underlying and volatility
- M61.15 — Attribution: exposure, carry, volatility, timing, sizing, execution and rule breaches
- M61.16 — Statistical uncertainty: sample size, overlapping trades, multiple comparisons and confidence
- M61.17 — Review cadence: daily operations, weekly behavior, monthly system and regime review
- M61.18 — Paper/live differences: fills, costs, capacity, behavior and performance decay
- M61.19 — Annualization and serial dependence: non-IID returns, overlapping trades, smoothed marks and distorted Sharpe ratios

Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Deleting losers or mixing process ratings with P&L.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- Why is Sharpe unstable for short histories with rare losses?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?
