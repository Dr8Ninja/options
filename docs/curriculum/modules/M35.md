# M35 — Realized volatility and forecasting

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `realized-volatility-and-forecasting`. Intermediate; 16–40 hours. Tags: volatility, volatility-and-volatility-trading.

Estimate realized volatility with explicit sampling, annualization and uncertainty.

Hard prerequisites: C-M11. Recommended preparation: C-M10, C-M12. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: How do overnight gaps alter a close-to-close versus intraday estimator? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Estimate realized volatility with explicit sampling, annualization and uncertainty.
- Solve and explain: Sample daily volatility 1% times sqrt 252 is 15.8745% annual under stated day convention.
- Audit the failure mode: Mixing log/simple returns, annualization bases or adjusted/raw prices.

Mathematics: A, S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R34:ARCH motivation; RX10:Bennett volatility chapters. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M35.01 — Variance versus volatility: squared returns, square roots and units
- M35.02 — Return measurement: simple/log, sampling horizon and overnight/intraday separation
- M35.03 — Annualization: trading/calendar days, scaling assumptions and comparability
- M35.04 — Close-to-close estimators: sample means, overlapping windows and measurement error
- M35.05 — Range estimators: Parkinson, Garman–Klass, Rogers–Satchell and Yang–Zhang assumptions
- M35.06 — High-frequency realized variance: summation, sampling frequency and microstructure noise
- M35.07 — Robust realized measures: realized kernels, subsampling and noise adjustment
- M35.08 — Jump separation: bipower variation, discontinuities and estimation limits
- M35.09 — Volatility clustering: persistence, leverage effect and changing regimes
- M35.10 — Volatility cones: matched horizons, distribution percentiles and sample contamination
- M35.11 — Forecast models: EWMA, GARCH, HAR, ensembles and benchmark comparison
- M35.12 — Forecast losses: squared error, QLIKE, uncertainty intervals and horizon alignment
- M35.13 — Expected realized variance: conditional forecast versus trailing historical estimate
- M35.14 — Seasonality: intraday U-shape, weekdays, holidays and event calendars
- M35.15 — Integrated versus realized variance: latent process, finite sampling and jumps
- M35.16 — Overnight and intraday variance allocation: nontrading time, holiday effects, event clocks and matched hedge horizons
