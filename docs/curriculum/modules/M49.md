# M49 — Operational, model and information risk

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `operational-model-and-information-risk`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: risk, risk-and-portfolio-construction.

Reconcile records and design containment, recovery and independent model challenge.

Hard prerequisites: C-M05, C-M22. Recommended preparation: C-M03, C-M04, C-M45. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: What should happen when internal and broker positions disagree? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Reconcile records and design containment, recovery and independent model challenge.
- Solve and explain: Internal 10 contracts broker 9: difference 1; freeze new exposure and investigate fills/corrections before guessing.
- Audit the failure mode: Blind resubmission after timeout; missing idempotency or stale risk snapshots.

Mathematics: A, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R48:BCP overview; R45:bank guidance boundary; R47:Knight case. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M49.01 — Operational loss taxonomy: wrong contracts, missing hedges, duplicates, timing and reconciliation
- M49.02 — Pre-trade controls: quantity, price collars, exposure caps and allowed instruments
- M49.03 — Kill switches: cancel-all, disable new risk, flattening limits and independent access
- M49.04 — Outage plans: internet, power, broker, exchange, data and backup communications
- M49.05 — Position reconciliation: orders versus fills versus broker positions and cash
- M49.06 — Corporate-action and expiry alerts: versioned metadata, deadlines and responsibility
- M49.07 — Model risk: specification, estimation, calibration, implementation and misuse
- M49.08 — Independent validation: conceptual soundness, benchmarking, outcomes and monitoring
- M49.09 — Data risk: stale feeds, dropped messages, incorrect clocks, bad corporate actions
- M49.10 — Cybersecurity: credentials, least privilege, read/trade permissions and secrets handling
- M49.11 — Vendor risk: data licensing, service continuity, opaque analytics and support
- M49.12 — Change management: versioning, review, rollback, staged deployment and audit trail
- M49.13 — Incident response: preserve evidence, contain loss, communicate, reconcile and prevent recurrence
- M49.14 — Business continuity: emergency procedures, access recovery and periodic drills

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Blind resubmission after timeout; missing idempotency or stale risk snapshots.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- What should happen when internal and broker positions disagree?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?

Mastery:

- Contain new risk, reconcile positions and restore operation using a documented procedure.
- At least 85% on quiz and rubric; no unresolved critical errors; solve a fresh transfer variant with reasoning.

Remediation: Diagnose the mistaken unit, model, observation or cash-flow step; revisit the cited entry bridge or source section, annotate the correction, then attempt an unseen variant after a separate review session.
