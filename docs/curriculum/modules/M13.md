# M13 — Calculus, linear algebra and numerical foundations

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `calculus-linear-algebra-and-numerical-foundations`. Intermediate; 13–34 hours. Tags: mathematics, mathematics-and-inference.

Differentiate, approximate, optimize and diagnose unstable numerical answers.

Hard prerequisites: C-B02. Recommended preparation: C-M10. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: When does a Taylor approximation fail despite correct derivatives? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Differentiate, approximate, optimize and diagnose unstable numerical answers.
- Solve and explain: For f(S)=S² at 100 and shock 2, linear predicts 400 change; quadratic adds 4 giving 404.
- Audit the failure mode: Mismatched units, step-size cancellation and unconstrained optimization.

Mathematics: C, L, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R05:19 and 21 TOC-based; R16:mathematical appendix. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M13.01 — Single-variable calculus: limits, derivatives, integrals and rate of change
- M13.02 — Multivariable calculus: partial derivatives, gradients, Hessians and chain rule
- M13.03 — Taylor expansion: local approximation, cross terms and residual error
- M13.04 — Vectors and matrices: linear systems, rank, conditioning, positive semidefiniteness
- M13.05 — Eigenvectors and PCA: covariance structure, factor reduction, interpretation limits
- M13.06 — Optimization: constrained/unconstrained, convexity, local versus global solutions
- M13.07 — Numerical root finding: bisection, Brent and Newton methods; convergence safeguards
- M13.08 — Interpolation: linear, spline, shape preservation and unstable extrapolation
- M13.09 — Numerical integration: quadrature, integration error and tail truncation
- M13.10 — Differential equations: ODEs, PDEs, boundary and terminal conditions
- M13.11 — Fourier and characteristic functions: transforms and pricing applications
- M13.12 — Numerical stability: floating point, cancellation, grids and convergence testing
- M13.13 — Automatic differentiation: forward/reverse modes, computational graphs and Greek calculation

Common mistakes:

- Mismatched units, step-size cancellation and unconstrained optimization.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- When does a Taylor approximation fail despite correct derivatives?
