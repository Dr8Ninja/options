# M24 — Single-leg, stock-linked and directional structures

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `single-leg-stock-linked-and-directional-structures`. Beginner; 16–40 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Calculate long/short single-leg, covered-call, protective-put and collar cash flows.

Hard prerequisites: C-M22. Recommended preparation: C-M21. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: What downside remains in a covered call? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Calculate long/short single-leg, covered-call, protective-put and collar cash flows.
- Solve and explain: Stock 100 plus short 105 call premium 2 at expiry 80: loss 18 per share; at 110: profit 7.
- Audit the failure mode: Calling covered calls income without downside exposure.

Mathematics: A (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:X; RX02:put-call parity. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M24.01 — Long call: bullish convexity, premium risk and volatility exposure
- M24.02 — Long put: bearish exposure, portfolio insurance and downside convexity
- M24.03 — Short call: uncovered upside exposure, margin and assignment
- M24.04 — Short put: downside obligation, collateral, gap and assignment
- M24.05 — Covered call: stock-plus-short-call exposure, capped upside and residual downside
- M24.06 — Protective put: stock-plus-put insurance, premium drag and expiry renewal
- M24.07 — Cash-secured put: funded acquisition obligation and comparison with covered calls
- M24.08 — Married put and protective call: timing, short-stock hedging and borrow risk
- M24.09 — Synthetic long stock: call/put construction, financing and exercise mismatch
- M24.10 — Synthetic short stock: call/put construction, collateral and assignment
- M24.11 — Collar: downside protection, upside sale, funding and strike tradeoffs
- M24.12 — Risk reversal: directional use versus smile quote; bullish and bearish constructions
- M24.13 — Covered straddle: stock plus short call/put, doubled downside exposure
- M24.14 — Stock replacement: deep ITM calls, leverage, dividend and funding differences
- M24.15 — LEAPS implementation: long horizon, spread, vega/rho, dividends and roll risk
- M24.16 — Wheel strategy: linked cash-secured puts and covered calls; path dependence and equity beta
