# M64 — Other jurisdictions and cross-border trading

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `other-jurisdictions-and-cross-border-trading`. Advanced; 22–45 hours. Tags: regulation-and-tax, rules-operations-and-market-specific-practice.

Compare jurisdiction and product conventions rather than exporting US assumptions.

Hard prerequisites: C-M42, C-M47. Recommended preparation: C-M03, C-M22. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Which legal entity, currency and calendar determine this obligation? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Compare jurisdiction and product conventions rather than exporting US assumptions.
- Solve and explain: USD1000 liability at FX83 costs 83000 INR; at 85 costs 85000 before conversion spread.
- Audit the failure mode: Ignoring cross-currency collateral, local holidays and counterparty terms.

Mathematics: A, C (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R43:Article 41; R46:Prisma; R63:spec page; R69:scope. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M64.01 — European Union branch: MiFID/MiFIR, EMIR, appropriateness, clearing and reporting scope
- M64.02 — United Kingdom branch: FCA rules, UK EMIR, product access and local divergence
- M64.03 — Continental exchange practice: Eurex contracts, Prisma, exercise and settlement calendars
- M64.04 — Hong Kong branch: HKEX/SFC, stock/index options, lots, settlement and trading sessions
- M64.05 — Singapore branch: SGX/MAS, futures options, clearing and cross-market links
- M64.06 — Australia and Japan branches: ASX/ASIC and JPX rules, styles, settlement and local calendars
- M64.07 — Cross-border eligibility: residency, permitted products, exchange controls and broker permissions
- M64.08 — Cross-border taxation: withholding, treaties, foreign-account reporting and currency conversion
- M64.09 — Client classification: retail/professional distinctions, disclosures and protection differences
- M64.10 — Legal entity and netting: insolvency, collateral location and enforceability
- M64.11 — Jurisdiction worksheet: official contract, regulator, broker, tax source and effective-date checks

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Ignoring cross-currency collateral, local holidays and counterparty terms.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
