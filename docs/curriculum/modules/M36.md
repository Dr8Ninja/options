# M36 — Implied volatility, distributions and risk premiums

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `implied-volatility-distributions-and-risk-premiums`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: volatility, volatility-and-volatility-trading.

Match implied and realized variance horizons and interpret risk premium cautiously.

Hard prerequisites: C-M35, C-M30. Recommended preparation: none. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why does a positive average variance spread not ensure a profitable retail trade? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Match implied and realized variance horizons and interpret risk premium cautiously.
- Solve and explain: IV20% RV18%: difference in annual variance .0076, not 2% variance.
- Audit the failure mode: Ignoring crash compensation, costs and overlapping observations.

Mathematics: S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R20:abstract only, empirical statistics excluded; R12:replication sections. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M36.01 — Implied volatility: inversion of a selected pricing model, not a direct forecast
- M36.02 — Implied versus realized: matched horizon, weighting, forecasting and risk compensation
- M36.03 — Volatility risk premium: expected compensation, tails, state dependence and definition
- M36.04 — Variance risk premium: pricing versus physical expected variance; distinction from volatility spread
- M36.05 — IV rank: range normalization, lookback and sensitivity to outlier extrema
- M36.06 — IV percentile: empirical ranking, ties, lookback and provider definitions
- M36.07 — Implied move: model scaling, straddle-implied breakeven and confidence-level distinctions
- M36.08 — Risk-neutral density: strike derivatives, smoothing, noisy wings and no-arbitrage
- M36.09 — Implied probability: pricing probabilities, investor beliefs and risk-premium wedge
- M36.10 — Variance risk versus jump risk: continuous variation, crash insurance and tail premiums
- M36.11 — Carry: theta, financing, expected hedging cost and risk compensation
- M36.12 — Volatility-of-volatility: forecast uncertainty, volga, dynamics and convexity
- M36.13 — Cheap versus expensive volatility: forecast, costs, hedge policy, uncertainty and alternatives
- M36.14 — Volatility regimes: level, persistence, skew, term structure and transition risk
