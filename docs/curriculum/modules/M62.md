# M62 — United States regulatory, tax and operational branch

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `united-states-regulatory-tax-and-operational-branch`. Intermediate; 16–40 hours. Tags: regulation-and-tax, rules-operations-and-market-specific-practice.

Locate US product rules, assignment procedures and account restrictions without generalizing.

Hard prerequisites: C-M22, C-M47. Recommended preparation: C-M03, C-M05. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why do SPX-style cash settlement and single-stock delivery need separate worksheets? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Locate US product rules, assignment procedures and account restrictions without generalizing.
- Solve and explain: A hypothetical index multiplier 100 and settlement 4100 versusstrike 4000 pays 10000; no stock delivery in this cash contract.
- Audit the failure mode: Assuming every index settles the same time or broker deadline equals exchange deadline.

Mathematics: A (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R01:IV/VIII/IX; R61:(g). Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M62.01 — Regulatory responsibilities: SEC, FINRA, CFTC, NFA, exchanges and OCC
- M62.02 — Broker/account permissions: options approval, cash/margin accounts and product restrictions
- M62.03 — Margin rules: Regulation T, FINRA/exchange requirements, portfolio margin and house rules
- M62.04 — Trading restrictions: current intraday margin/day-trading rules, settled funds and account violations
- M62.05 — Exercise and assignment: current thresholds, broker cutoffs, contrary instructions and allocation
- M62.06 — Contract differences: equity/ETF/index options, American/European, AM/PM and cash/physical
- M62.07 — Position and exercise limits: aggregation, reporting, exemptions and product specifications
- M62.08 — Short-sale rules: locate, delivery, buy-ins, restricted securities and borrow
- M62.09 — Best execution and routing: order handling, payment for order flow and disclosure
- M62.10 — Market conduct: insider trading, manipulation, spoofing, wash trades and information barriers
- M62.11 — Tax classification: equity versus qualifying nonequity options, Section 1256 and instrument-specific treatment
- M62.12 — Tax interactions: straddles, wash sales, qualified covered calls and constructive sales
- M62.13 — Trader status and elections: business status, mark-to-market elections and qualified advice
- M62.14 — Tax records: Form 8949, Schedule D, Form 6781 where applicable; basis and exercise reconciliation
- M62.15 — Investor protections: broker/FCM segregation, custody, complaint channels and protection limits
- M62.16 — Exercise and account cash settlement interaction: broker closeouts, residual positions, current settlement cycle and insufficient-funds procedures
