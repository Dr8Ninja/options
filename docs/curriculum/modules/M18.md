# M18 — Trend indicators

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `trend-indicators`. Intermediate; 13–34 hours. Tags: technical-analysis, price-behavior-and-technical-methods.

Measure trend with a fixed rule and quantify lag and whipsaw costs.

Hard prerequisites: C-M15. Recommended preparation: C-M10. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Does a crossover indicate a profitable forward distribution? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Measure trend with a fixed rule and quantify lag and whipsaw costs.
- Solve and explain: Prices 98,100,102: simple 3-period mean 100; last price is 2 above mean.
- Audit the failure mode: Optimizing many windows on the evaluation sample.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R54:original-paper data and scope; R29:critical reading. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M18.01 — Simple moving average: window, lag and smoothing
- M18.02 — Exponential moving average: decay, initialization and effective memory
- M18.03 — Weighted moving average: linear weights and responsiveness
- M18.04 — Volume-weighted moving average: volume dependence and VWAP distinction
- M18.05 — Moving-average crossovers: slow/fast windows, whipsaw and turnover
- M18.06 — Slope and distance measures: normalization, z-scores and trend strength
- M18.07 — ADX and directional movement: plus/minus DI, smoothing and non-directional strength
- M18.08 — Aroon: time since highs/lows, range sensitivity and reversals
- M18.09 — Supertrend: ATR bands, direction switching and parameter sensitivity
- M18.10 — Ichimoku cloud: component lines, displacement, look-ahead plotting traps
- M18.11 — Parabolic SAR: acceleration, stop placement and range-market failure
- M18.12 — Adaptive averages: KAMA, DEMA, TEMA, Hull moving average; complexity costs
- M18.13 — Trend-indicator comparison: equivalent filters, correlated signals, regime dependence and costs
