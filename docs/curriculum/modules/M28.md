# M28 — Synthetics, financing and constrained arbitrage

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `synthetics-financing-and-constrained-arbitrage`. Advanced; 24–48 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Use parity to analyze synthetics, boxes and conversion/reversal after funding and borrow.

Hard prerequisites: C-M29, C-M22. Recommended preparation: C-M25, C-M05. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can a quoted box yield fail to be a free arbitrage? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Use parity to analyze synthetics, boxes and conversion/reversal after funding and borrow.
- Solve and explain: European 100/110 box pays 10 at common maturity; price 9.8 implies 2.0408% period return before costs.
- Audit the failure mode: Ignoring American exercise, stock borrow, collateral and settlement mismatch.

Mathematics: A, C (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: RX02:parity; R05:11. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M28.01 — Synthetic forwards: put-call combinations, strike cash flow and discounting
- M28.02 — Conversion: stock plus protective put and short call; cash-flow replication
- M28.03 — Reversal: reverse conversion, stock borrow and operational feasibility
- M28.04 — Long box spread: European-style terminal cash flow and implied financing rate
- M28.05 — Short box spread: borrowing interpretation, collateral, exercise and credit constraints
- M28.06 — American box hazards: early assignment, funding and non-simultaneous legs
- M28.07 — Put-call parity trades: borrow, dividends, rates, costs and executable bounds
- M28.08 — Dividend-implied trades: discrete distributions, exercise, tax and estimation risk
- M28.09 — Jelly roll: two synthetic forwards, implied carry and term financing
- M28.10 — Reversal/conversion diagnostics: apparent arbitrage from stale or asynchronous quotes
- M28.11 — Static replication: portfolios of vanilla claims, digitals and state-price densities
- M28.12 — Arbitrage limits: capital, financing, access, latency, settlement and model assumptions
