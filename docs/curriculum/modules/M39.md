# M39 — Volatility indices, derivatives and exchange-traded products

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `volatility-indices-derivatives-and-exchange-traded-products`. Advanced; 26–51 hours. Tags: volatility, volatility-and-volatility-trading.

Distinguish VIX spot methodology, settlement, futures basis and rolling-product returns.

Hard prerequisites: C-M36. Recommended preparation: C-M37. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why cannot spot VIX be bought and held directly? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Distinguish VIX spot methodology, settlement, futures basis and rolling-product returns.
- Solve and explain: Two variance estimates .04/.09 weighted .5/.5 imply volatility sqrt(.065)=25.495%, not 25%.
- Audit the failure mode: Averaging volatilities; treating VIX ETPs as spot; ignoring acceleration terms.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.
