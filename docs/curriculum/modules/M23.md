# M23 — Option-chain analysis and inference limits

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `option-chain-analysis-and-inference-limits`. Beginner; 16–40 hours. Tags: options, option-contracts-and-structures.

Reject stale or non-executable chain observations and select comparable contracts.

Hard prerequisites: C-M04, C-M22. Recommended preparation: C-M21, C-M10. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Does high open interest guarantee a tight executable quote now? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Reject stale or non-executable chain observations and select comparable contracts.
- Solve and explain: Two same-contract quotes at 10:00 and 10:05 cannot form a simultaneous arbitrage; flag a crossed 2.2/2.0 market.
- Audit the failure mode: Mismatching underlying timestamp, expiry, multiplier or adjusted deliverable.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R57:contract directory; R51:data overview only. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M23.01 — Chain layout: strikes, expiries, calls/puts, symbols, multiplier and currency
- M23.02 — Quote fields: bid, ask, size, midpoint, last, timestamp and crossed/stale quotes
- M23.03 — Volume versus open interest: transactions versus outstanding contracts; publication timing
- M23.04 — Open-interest changes: opening/closing combinations, spread legs and bilateral positions
- M23.05 — IV and Greek fields: model inputs, conventions, provider differences and missing values
- M23.06 — Liquidity assessment: spread, depth, actual fills, activity, concentration and quote stability
- M23.07 — Strike selection: exposure, liquidity, strike spacing and forward moneyness
- M23.08 — Expiry selection: horizon, events, theta/gamma balance and available contracts
- M23.09 — Trade classification: bid/ask inference, complex trades, auctions, reporting delays
- M23.10 — Unusual activity: size versus baseline, roll detection, stock hedges and inference uncertainty
- M23.11 — Put-call ratios: volume/OI, equity/index, expiry, strike and hedging distortions
- M23.12 — Max pain: settlement-payoff calculation versus unsupported forecasting claims
- M23.13 — Open-interest walls: descriptive concentrations versus guaranteed support/resistance
- M23.14 — Gamma exposure estimates: assumed dealer signs, hedge instrument and data coverage
- M23.15 — Expected-move estimates: straddle cost, IV scaling, quantiles and distribution assumptions
- M23.16 — Chain sanity checks: parity, monotonicity, convexity, calendars and bad-input diagnosis

Common mistakes:

- Mismatching underlying timestamp, expiry, multiplier or adjusted deliverable.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
