# M37 — Smiles, skew, term structure and the surface

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `smiles-skew-term-structure-and-the-surface`. Advanced; 30–57 hours. Tags: volatility, volatility-and-volatility-trading.

Build a surface in forward moneyness and total variance with arbitrage diagnostics.

Hard prerequisites: C-M36, C-M31. Recommended preparation: C-M29, C-M32. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Is positive total variance sufficient for an arbitrage-free smile? Bridge: B02.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Build a surface in forward moneyness and total variance with arbitrage diagnostics.
- Solve and explain: 20% IV at.25y givesw=.01;18% at 1y gives.0324; positive calendar difference alone does not check butterfly arbitrage.
- Audit the failure mode: Unconstrained interpolation; mixing American quotes with European formulas.

Mathematics: C, N, I (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: B03 plus arrays, functions and tests.

Reading: R35:sections 2–5; RX04:sections 3–4 and conclusion. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M37.01 — Smile and smirk: implied volatility across strike and economic interpretations
- M37.02 — Put and call skew: downside/upside asymmetry, supply/demand and jump risk
- M37.03 — Moneyness coordinates: strike, spot/forward moneyness, log-moneyness and delta
- M37.04 — Term structure: maturity-dependent IV, events, carry and mean reversion
- M37.05 — Total implied variance: maturity normalization and calendar comparisons
- M37.06 — Forward variance and volatility: total-variance differencing, annualization and assumptions
- M37.07 — Surface construction: synchronized quotes, forwards, rates, dividends and exercise model
- M37.08 — Surface interpolation: strike/expiry grids, sparse wings, liquidity and smoothing
- M37.09 — Static arbitrage: vertical, butterfly and calendar consistency in appropriate coordinates
- M37.10 — Smile dynamics: sticky strike, sticky delta, sticky moneyness and empirical behavior
- M37.11 — Spot-volatility relationship: leverage effect, correlation and skew movement
- M37.12 — Surface factors: level, slope, curvature, term factors and PCA
- M37.13 — Delta-space quotes: risk reversals, butterflies and market-specific delta conventions
- M37.14 — Surface uncertainty: bid/ask IV, calibration stability and extrapolation risk
- M37.15 — Calendar skew terminology: term slope versus strike-skew evolution; define usage
- M37.P01 — American-quote treatment: Identify exercise premium and discrete dividends; compare direct American-model calibration with de-Americanization; bound error, preserve bid/ask and exercise-model uncertainty.
