# M54 — Market data and research engineering

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `market-data-and-research-engineering`. Intermediate; 16–40 hours. Tags: quantitative, research-data-and-software.

Build point-in-time data with traceable identifiers, timestamps, adjustments and rights.

Hard prerequisites: C-B03, C-M23. Recommended preparation: C-M03, C-M11, C-M21. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why cannot today's survivor universe be used as yesterday's opportunity set? Bridge: B03.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Build point-in-time data with traceable identifiers, timestamps, adjustments and rights.
- Solve and explain: Quote dated 10:01 for decision 10:00 is unavailable; reject rather than shift silently.
- Audit the failure mode: Stale marks, revised contracts, timezone errors and licensed-data redistribution.

Mathematics: A, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: B03 plus arrays, functions and tests.

Reading: R18:real-time periods; R51:vendor scope; R55:time-series guide. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M54.01 — Data provenance: vendor, entitlement, license, methodology and coverage
- M54.02 — Point-in-time data: availability timestamp, revisions, restatements and historical membership
- M54.03 — Time handling: UTC/local zones, daylight savings, sessions and exchange calendars
- M54.04 — Quotes and trades: NBBO, direct feeds, depth, conditions and timestamps
- M54.05 — Option identifiers: underlying mapping, adjusted symbols, expiry, strike, type and multiplier
- M54.06 — Corporate actions: split adjustment, dividends, deliverables and delisting returns
- M54.07 — Interest and borrow data: curve timestamp, dividend forecasts and securities lending
- M54.08 — Synchronization: asynchronous chains, stale underlyings and false arbitrage
- M54.09 — Missing/bad data: zero bids, crossed quotes, duplicate trades, outliers and corrections
- M54.10 — Survivorship: expired options, dead underlyings, contract histories and selection rules
- M54.11 — Event databases: earnings timestamps, revisions, surprise measures and after-hours releases
- M54.12 — Historical rules: changing lots, taxes, expiry calendars, margin and trading hours
- M54.13 — Data storage: schemas, SQL, columnar files, partitions, lineage and versioning
- M54.14 — Quality controls: checksums, invariant tests, audit samples and documented exclusions
- M54.15 — Dataset economics: coverage versus cost, sample limitations and research feasibility
- M54.16 — Historical data entitlements and revisions: vendor backfills, methodology changes, survivorship in downloadable chains and reproducible snapshots

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Stale marks, revised contracts, timezone errors and licensed-data redistribution.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.

Checkpoint questions:

- Why cannot today's survivor universe be used as yesterday's opportunity set?
- Which assumption in your numerical result is most vulnerable, and what changes if it fails?

Mastery:

- Produce an auditable data dictionary, exclusion log and timestamp checks.
- At least 85% on quiz and rubric; no unresolved critical errors; solve a fresh transfer variant with reasoning.

Remediation: Diagnose the mistaken unit, model, observation or cash-flow step; revisit the cited entry bridge or source section, annotate the correction, then attempt an unseen variant after a separate review session.
