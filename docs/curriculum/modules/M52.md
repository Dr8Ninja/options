# M52 — Professional order execution and cost analysis

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `professional-order-execution-and-cost-analysis`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: execution, microstructure-execution-and-management.

Benchmark execution against arrival price and model fill probabilities and legging risk.

Hard prerequisites: C-M50, C-M05. Recommended preparation: C-M04, C-M21. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can the lowest commission route have the worst net result? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Benchmark execution against arrival price and model fill probabilities and legging risk.
- Solve and explain: Buy 100 shares at 100.1 versus arrival 100 plusfee 2: implementation shortfall 12.
- Audit the failure mode: Midpoint fills, independent perfect multi-leg fills and zero impact.

Mathematics: A, S, N (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R30:complex order mechanics; R50:abstract only. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M52.01 — Executable valuation: bid/ask, sizes, package quotes and realistic liquidation marks
- M52.02 — Limit-price selection: urgency, spread, expected adverse selection and non-fill cost
- M52.03 — Multi-leg execution: net debit/credit, legging risk, ratios and partial fills
- M52.04 — Price-improvement auctions: eligibility, access, competing responses and fees
- M52.05 — Smart routing: venue choice, fill quality, rebates and conflicts
- M52.06 — Transaction-cost analysis: decision/arrival price, implementation shortfall and benchmarks
- M52.07 — Effective and realized spreads: fill price, midquote timing and information effects
- M52.08 — Slippage models: spread, size, time, volatility, events and adverse conditions
- M52.09 — VWAP, TWAP and participation: benchmark suitability, urgency and option-liquidity limits
- M52.10 — Optimal execution: risk/impact tradeoff, Almgren–Chriss and model assumptions
- M52.11 — Queue-aware execution: cancellation, repricing, information leakage and fill estimates
- M52.12 — Order monitoring: acknowledgment, rejection, partial fill, cancel confirmation and reconciliation
- M52.13 — Event and expiry execution: widening spreads, halts, auctions and last-trade constraints
- M52.14 — Execution review: per-leg/package costs, missed trades and strategy-level net edge

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
