# M19 — Momentum indicators

Status: learning design; topic lessons remain P17. Slug: `momentum-indicators`. Intermediate; 14–36 hours. Tags: technical-analysis, price-behavior-and-technical-methods.

Compute bounded momentum indicators and evaluate their conditional relevance.

Hard prerequisites: C-M15. Recommended preparation: C-M18. Optional enrichment: none. Diagnostic: Why can overbought persist during a strong trend? Bridge: B01.

Estimation basis: Editorial estimate for selected readings, scope navigation, one worked calculation, one transfer variant, practical work and remediation; 45–90 minutes per ordinary scope topic plus 2–8 practical hours, longer for specialist derivation. Projects budgeted separately. Not measured learner time.

Objectives:

- Compute bounded momentum indicators and evaluate their conditional relevance.
- Solve and explain: RS=3 gives RSI=100-100/(1+3)=75 under the specified smoothing inputs.
- Audit the failure mode: Treating a threshold as a universal reversal law.

Mathematics: A, S (codes in MATH_ROADMAP). Programming: None for core explanations; optional Python for replication.

Reading: R27:indicator reference, full text not reviewed. Resource verification limits control use; a TOC assignment is a candidate, not a reviewed chapter. All learners can complete the synthetic numerical case without buying a book.

Ordered scope:

- M19.01 — RSI: gain/loss smoothing, thresholds, trend ranges and failure swings
- M19.02 — MACD: EMA differences, signal line, histogram and redundant smoothing
- M19.03 — Stochastic oscillator: close-in-range, fast/slow variants and smoothing
- M19.04 — Stochastic RSI: oscillator-of-oscillator, amplified noise and normalization
- M19.05 — Rate of change: percentage return over a lookback and time-scale dependence
- M19.06 — Momentum: raw price difference versus percentage normalization
- M19.07 — Commodity Channel Index: typical price, mean deviation and scaling
- M19.08 — Williams percent R: close-in-range inversion and stochastic redundancy
- M19.09 — True Strength Index: double smoothing and lag
- M19.10 — PPO and DPO: relative MACD and detrending; centered-data leakage
- M19.11 — Ultimate oscillator and Connors RSI: multiple horizons, ranking and overfitting
- M19.12 — Divergence: regular/hidden definitions, confirmation and hindsight bias
- M19.13 — Overbought/oversold: persistent trends, thresholds and conditioning on regime
- M19.14 — Momentum evidence: cross-sectional versus time-series momentum; indicator-specific claims

Teaching brief for module delivery: begin with the checkpoint dilemma, elicit the learner's units and obligations, work the numerical case, then use the practical assignment to challenge the main assumption. Ask for a counterexample before extending the scope. This is a module delivery plan, not a topic lesson.

Common mistakes:

- Treating a threshold as a universal reversal law.
- Accepting a result without its units, assumptions or source date.
