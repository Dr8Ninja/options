# Practice without required real-money trading

Practice begins with obligations and proceeds only when the prior gate is demonstrated. The budgets below are teaching scenarios, not personalized account recommendations. A learner can always choose zero exposure; a minimum contract larger than the stress budget is a correct reason to reject a trade.

| Level | Work product | Risk/cost constraints and gate |
|---|---|---|
|1. Cash and payoff | Paper tables for rights, units, long/short calls/puts, stock-linked and vertical positions | Hypothetical multiplier explicitly supplied; premium and payoff separated; no unresolved cash/assignment/loss-bound error. |
|2. Synthetic models | PR01–PR07, including invalid quotes, jumps and exercise branches | No market-data license required. Compare hand reference; price residual alone is insufficient when vega is near zero. |
|3. Historical reconstruction | M66 evidence packs: chronology, exposures, funding and competing explanations | Separate contemporary facts from hindsight. No invented intraday prices or exact positions. Missing data remains missing. |
|4. Paper positions | Timestamped thesis and hypothetical fills, daily cash/risk reconciliation | Declare capital 10000 units; example per-position stress budget 100 and total stress budget 500; these are arbitrary training limits. Reject size below one lot, maintain a cash buffer, and stop new exposure at a defined breach. |
|5. Rule-based simulation | PR08, assignment/settlement, outages and corporate actions | Base case crosses observable spread unless a validated fill model supports otherwise; fees on every leg; borrow/funding/taxes effective-dated; partial fills and non-fills recorded; stressed spread doubled and funding/margin increased. No unlimited borrowing. |
|6. Statistical evaluation | PR09–PR12 and capstones C03/C05/C06/C07 | Predeclare hypothesis, horizon, universe, baseline, falsification, search count and holdout. Report uncertainty, tail months, failed variants and capacity limits. Rejecting the strategy can pass. |

Every strategy dossier states direction, volatility expectation, horizon, skew, term structure, event exposure, liquidity and tail risk, convexity and carry. It specifies signed legs, initial and conditional future cash flows, thesis, expected edge, contrary evidence, regime assumptions, execution, sizing, assignment/dividend/funding paths, adjustment/exit rules and evaluation. No name such as “income,” “arbitrage” or “hedged” substitutes for this analysis.

The journal records what was knowable at the decision time; forecast probabilities/ranges; alternative of no trade; position and Greek units; intended/actual hypothetical fill; fees/funding; adverse paths; rule deviations; emotion/behavior observations; result; and post-review action. Record missed and rejected trades as well as executed ones. A weekly process review checks consistency and repeated mistakes; a less frequent statistical review asks whether evidence changed, avoiding a new strategy after every loss.

Operational drills include a stale feed, duplicate submission after timeout, mismatch with a broker-like statement, exercise of only one spread leg, a funding transfer delay and market closure during attempted liquidation. Contain new risk, reconcile authoritative records and document recovery. A stop price is not a guaranteed exit during a gap. Do not erase a loss by “rolling” or resetting the journal.

Assess process and outcome separately. A well-followed rule can lose; an undisciplined decision can win. Neither one observation nor paper profits establish an edge. Statistical evaluation must include the entire decision record and realistic implementation costs.
