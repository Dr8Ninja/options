# Content, evidence and maintenance guide

Three content states are deliberately distinct: **scope outline** names the boundaries; **teaching brief** specifies the explanation, example, misconception and assessment plan; **reviewed lesson** contains the authored instruction and reviewed assessment evidence. P01 has module learning designs and a few bounded new teaching briefs, but no fully authored reviewed topic lessons. Do not promote scope by changing a label.

A lesson follows the original delivery sequence: concept, intuition, mathematics, example, options relevance, practical application, failure modes, professional perspective, exercises, quiz and mastery check. The sequence is adapted to the topic; empty ceremonial headings do not improve teaching. State what the learner can do, why it matters, necessary preparation, exact symbols/units, a worked unfamiliar case, a counterexample, practice with reference behavior and a fresh transfer task.

Claims carry one of: established definition/result under assumptions; empirical finding with sample/method; practitioner heuristic; contested explanation; curriculum recommendation; current rule; or conflicted/unverified claim. Cite the exact section that supports the claim. A chapter assignment, author reputation, publisher blurb, abstract, directory link or HTTP200 is not a verified claim. Every empirical lesson needs dates/universe, selection and measurement limitations, uncertainty and plausible competing explanations. A famous trade needs a defensible chronology and exposure mechanism; otherwise omit the anecdote.

Editorial states: draft → subject review → pedagogy/accessibility review → reference/numerical review → ready for the chosen release. If evidence becomes stale/conflicting, mark needs review and exclude affected operational claims until resolved. Retire from a path without deleting the archival record; preserve stable IDs, replacements, rationale and a migration note for any eventual learner evidence. These are editorial requirements, not an application data-model decision.

Each correction log records date, affected IDs, old/new claim, evidence, reviewer role, verification scope and downstream impact. Separate code correctness, empirical replication and lesson comprehension checks. An agent’s three perspective passes are not independent human expert review; professional or legal certification is not implied.

Resource handling: retain bibliographic metadata and original annotations; link to lawful sources; do not host paywalled books or licensed market data without permission. Free-to-read does not mean free-to-redistribute. Author-created examples and research metadata are distributable only under the project’s eventual licensing choice; none of the source books’ licenses transfer to these exports. Raw temporary research downloads stay outside `data/`.

Publication gates reject: a missing safety explanation, an unanswered critical cash/assignment mistake, a factual claim supported only by a snippet, a current rule without a resolved applicable date/chain, an inaccessible mandatory reading without a usable alternative, or an implementation claim without a reference check. All current-rule quarantines and all outline-only lessons are excluded from first publication. P02 determines exactly which finished subset is a release; P17 performs the remaining authoring and review.

Effort tracking records actual reading, practice, remediation and review time separately. Initial ranges are editorial estimates; revise after pilot observations and diagnostic credit. Do not report a multi-year professional mastery claim as a fixed course duration or promise of profitable trading.
