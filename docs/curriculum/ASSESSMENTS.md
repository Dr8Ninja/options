# Assessment policy and usable practice

Every module has three original/canonical exercise records: a conceptual checkpoint, a numerical case with reference behavior, and the preserved practical assignment. Every module also has a four-part quiz blueprint. A blueprint is not a validated item bank. Numerical references are editorial answers, not claims that all practical projects have been implemented. Fresh variants, accessible delivery and item calibration remain P17 work.

The default rubric allocates 35% correctness, 25% units/assumptions, 25% failure analysis and 15% communication. Project rubrics additionally score lineage and reproducibility. The inherited 85% threshold is retained as a pedagogical default. Any unresolved error in units, cash obligations, assignment, leverage, loss bounds, leakage or data rights blocks demonstrated mastery regardless of total. All attempts, remediation and changed rubric versions must remain visible; this is a learning policy, not a decided application schema.

A worked starter: buy one hypothetical 100-strike European call for 3 with multiplier 100. If settlement is110, terminal payoff is1000 and net profit before costs is700. If settlement is95, payoff is0 and loss is300. Changing the multiplier to65 changes these figures to650/455 and 0/−195. It does not change the per-unit breakeven 103. A learner who calculates profit 1000, assumes the multiplier is universally 100, or omits an assigned short leg has not passed the safety gate.

A second transfer task: buy the 100/105 call vertical for a 2-unit debit. Terminal profit per unit ranges from−2 to3. Now make its short leg American with a dividend/exercise event and ask for interim stock/cash obligations. The terminal payoff bound alone does not demonstrate ability to fund or manage that path. The answer must name the resulting position and the available action under the actual contract/broker procedure, or explicitly state that the necessary current rule is unavailable.

Numerical criteria: exact integer cash arithmetic; stated rounding for decimals; benchmark BSM call/put tolerance 1e−7; finite-difference tolerances justified by a step sweep; Monte Carlo results assessed against confidence intervals and convergence, not an arbitrary exact match. A deterministic seed makes an experiment reproducible but does not measure its sampling uncertainty.

The [twelve projects](PROJECTS.md) have individual references, failure cases and rubrics. The seven original capstones are individually preserved in [capstones.json](../../data/v1/capstones.json), linked to projects and common policy POL-01. All 23 original decision-card questions remain individually addressable in [decision_cards.json](../../data/v1/decision_cards.json). Every capstone response must reconcile the strategy thesis, cash, risk, execution, evidence and rejection criteria rather than answer those questions in isolation.

Remediation is diagnosis → corrected explanation → worked counterexample → unseen transfer task. Repeating a memorized question cannot establish mastery. A diagnostic may grant entry credit but later failures reopen that prerequisite. Separate viewed, self-completed, attempted and demonstrated mastery. A profitable trade, real-money participation, a book purchase or a video completion is not assessment evidence.

Accessibility: offer plain-language prompts, semantic formulas with symbol definitions, data tables alongside graphs, calculator/paper alternatives for foundational skills, and untimed review where speed is not the competency. Coding competencies still require runnable work; a noncoding audit can demonstrate conceptual understanding without claiming programming proficiency. Language or motor demands must not become hidden proxies for mathematical understanding.
